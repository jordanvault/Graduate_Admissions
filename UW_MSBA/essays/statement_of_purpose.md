# Statement of Purpose (max 300 words) — 초안 v0.1, 303 words

> 참고용 초안. 본인 말로 다시 쓰고 AI 사용 공개에 반영.

After thirteen years in airline revenue management and logistics analytics, I have learned that analytics creates value only when it answers the right business question. I want the MSBA to give me the technical depth to answer those questions myself, and the leadership skills to make the answers matter.

At Korean Air, I ran forecasting and pricing systems and used Python and SQL to segment COVID-era route recovery and to build a monitoring tool that prevented an estimated $370,000 in annual revenue leakage. At Samsung SDS, I led a six-person task force that standardized how six regions and ten organizations counted freight volume, recovering 28,306 FEU of misclassified volume. Most of this work sits at the reporting and diagnostic end of the analytics spectrum. I now want to move toward prediction and strategic recommendation, which requires formal training I do not yet have.

My learning goals are predictive modeling and machine learning, causal inference to tell whether a pricing change caused a demand shift or merely coincided with it, and the judgment to know when a model deserves trust. I am equally interested in communication, because analytics most often fails at the handoff between a model and the people who must act on it.

Foster's approach fits this need. Starting from important business questions and then choosing the right analytical tools is how I already work, and the program's emphasis on leadership and on solving unstructured, real-world problems in team-based projects is what I want to build.

Within five years, I aim to work on pricing, capacity, or network decisions in a U.S. logistics or supply chain analytics team, or in a consulting practice serving those industries. Within ten years, I want to lead an analytics function between operations and executive leadership, accountable for data definitions, model design, and the decisions they inform.
