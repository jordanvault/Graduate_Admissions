# Personal Background and Growth Statement (optional, max 300 words) — 초안 v0.1

> 참고용 초안. SOP와 중복되지 않게 배경·성장 중심. ❓ 부분은 본인 이야기로 채워야 함.

My path has not been a straight line. I began my undergraduate studies at the University of Wisconsin–Madison in 2007 and paused them in 2009 for mandatory military service in the Korean Army. Returning in 2011, I spent 2012 as an exchange student at the National University of Singapore and finished my degree in 2013. Those years taught me how to work with people whose assumptions differ sharply from mine, a skill I have used ever since.

That habit shaped my career. At Korean Air, I worked across pricing, crisis recovery, and cross-border operations, including a nine-month project to merge more than 100 million records with Delta Air Lines' data for our transpacific joint venture. Both teams were reasonable and both used different definitions of the same customer. The work was not technical at first; it was patient conversation until we agreed on what each word meant.

In 2024, after eleven years in one industry, I moved to Samsung SDS to learn logistics. Starting again in a new field, with colleagues who knew the domain better than I did, was humbling, and it taught me to ask questions before offering answers. That approach let me see that six regions were not making errors but applying different, reasonable rules.

I would bring to the MSBA community an outsider's habit of making definitions explicit, thirteen years of decisions made under real constraints, and comfort with international teams across Korea, Singapore, and the United States. I also expect to learn a great deal from classmates who come from finance, engineering, and marketing.
